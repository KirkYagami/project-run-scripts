LoanServe - Swagger paste sheet

Each block: the endpoint to open in Swagger, which token must be authorised,
what to fill in, and what you should get back.
In Swagger: open the endpoint -> Try it out -> paste -> Execute.

Start: PORT=3002 ROOT_PATH=/proxy/3002 ./run_loanserve.sh --skip-tests  ->  open /proxy/3002/docs
Data is kept in memory: restart the service for a clean run (IDs start at LA000001).
