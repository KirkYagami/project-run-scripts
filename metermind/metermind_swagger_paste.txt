MeterMind - Swagger paste sheet

Each block: the endpoint to open in Swagger, which token must be authorised,
what to fill in, and what you should get back.
In Swagger: open the endpoint -> Try it out -> paste -> Execute.

Start: PORT=3001 ROOT_PATH=/proxy/3001 ./run_metermind.sh --skip-tests  ->  open /proxy/3001/docs
Readings are saved in SQLite, so YOUR reading_id values may differ from the ones below:
copy the reading_id from each Submit response (readings A, B and C) and use those.
Accounts are kept in memory: after a restart, sign up again.

==============================================================================
